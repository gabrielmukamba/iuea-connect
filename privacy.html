Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

IUEA Connect is a student-built platform and is not an official IUEA system.

Your account

When you join, we store your name, email address and password with Google Firebase Authentication. Your email address and password are never shown to other members. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are not visible to the founder.

Your profile

You can add a profile photo, your faculty and a short bio. Your name, photo, faculty and bio are shown to other signed-in members on the Members page, and your name and photo appear next to your chat messages, posts and comments. Posts and comments are also visible to visitors who are not signed in. Profile photos are stored with Cloudinary. You can change or edit your profile at any time, and you can ask for it to be removed.

What you share

Posts, comments, chat messages, photos, videos, documents and shared locations are visible to other members, and posts and comments are also visible to visitors. They are stored with Google Firebase and Cloudinary. Please do not share passwords, ID numbers or other private details. Share your location only if you are comfortable with everyone seeing it.

Discover: news, search and AI

Headlines come from BBC News. The search box is provided by Google and may collect usage data under Google's own privacy policy. When you ask the AI assistant a question, the text you type is sent to Google's Gemini service to produce an answer, and Google may use it to improve its products. Please do not type personal information, and check important answers with official sources because AI can make mistakes.

Member count

The site counts how many accounts have joined. Only the number is shown publicly.

On your device

Your browser keeps you logged in so you do not have to sign in each time.

How it is used

To connect students. We do not sell data or run advertising.

Removal

Email gabrielmukamba7@gmail.com to have your account, profile, a post or a message deleted.
