Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

IUEA Connect is a student-built platform and is not an official IUEA system.

What we collect

Only what you type into the community forms and chat: your name, faculty, post text, a contact email for study groups, and chat messages. Please do not type phone numbers, passwords or other private details.

Who can see it

Posts, study groups and chat messages are shown publicly on the Community page. Chat messages are stored with Google Firebase.

On your device

Your browser keeps a random ID and the name you used in the chat, so your own messages appear on the right.

How it is used

To connect students. We do not sell data or run advertising.

Removal

Email gabrielmukamba7@gmail.com to have a post or message removed.
