Why I built this

I have always believed that meaningful innovation begins with seeing possibilities where others see limitations.

IUEA Connect was born from a deeper question: What if technology could become more than a tool for communication — what if it could become an instrument of unity, opportunity, and institutional transformation?

My vision extends beyond writing code or developing digital platforms. I am fascinated by the power of ideas, the architecture of systems, and the possibilities that emerge when creativity meets purpose.

I contested Mr IUEA 2026 with a vision to contribute something meaningful. That vision did not end with the competition. It evolved into a commitment to build something that could outlive a moment, a position, or a title.

IUEA Connect represents that commitment: an initiative driven by innovation, intellectual curiosity, and a genuine desire to serve the university community.

I don't simply want to participate in the future of technology. I want to contribute to building it.

— Gabriel MukambaFounder, IUEA Connect
