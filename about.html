About IUEA

A chartered private university in Kampala. Quality education with practical skills.

Who we are

The International University of East Africa is a private, non-profit university in Kansanga, Kampala, chartered by Uganda's National Council for Higher Education. IUEA's applicant portal describes the university as established since 2011 and home to a community of over 15,000 students across East Africa.

Vision

To be the Technological University of Choice in Africa.

Mission

To provide an excellent education including practical experience and skills.

Motto

"Learning to Succeed".
