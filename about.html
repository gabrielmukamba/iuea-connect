About IUEA

A chartered private university in Kampala.

Who we are

The International University of East Africa is a private, non-profit university in Kansanga, Kampala, chartered by Uganda's National Council for Higher Education. It was established in 2010 and its first graduates passed out in 2014.

Vision

To be the Technological University of Choice in Africa.

Mission

To provide an excellent education including practical experience and skills.

Motto

"Learning to Succeed".
